# FloWave Privacy Policy (Draft)

This offline copy is a general implementation-based draft for review, not legal advice. The maintained source document is `docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md` in the repository.

FloWave stores library metadata, URI references, playback state, preferences, playlists, listening statistics, diagnostics and crash reports locally. It does not require an account or intentionally send the local library, playlists or diagnostics to a FloWave server.

Online search, artwork, lyrics, stream resolution and downloads use network clients and third-party endpoints. Android media/storage, Internet and foreground-media permissions support these functions. Diagnostic logs redact credentials, cookies, token-like values and URL query parameters before persistence; exports are controlled by the user.

This draft must be legally reviewed and updated before public release.
